Find your next Volunteering session at UCL, all in one place

The UCL Volunteering Society (VolSoc) app brings together Volunteering events from societies across UCL, so you can see what's on, pick something that fits your week and turn up

WHAT'S ON
A week view and a list of upcoming Volunteering events from VolSoc, UCL Student Social Impact and UCL's social impact societies, like Street Aid, Red Cross and Student Action for Refugees
Filter by society, open an event for its time, place and sign-up link, and add it to your own calendar
Subscribe to the whole feed so new events turn up in the calendar app you already use

VOLUNTEER WITH US
Put your name on VolSoc's volunteer list with your UCL login
Tell us how often you can help, when in the year and when in a typical week you're free, and what you'd like to do, from food rescue and outreach to gardening, tutoring and befriending
Come back and change your answers any time, or take yourself off the list

ZERO FOOD WASTE
Shift leaders log what they collected straight after a shift: the date, the outlet and a count of each kind of food
No sign-in needed, and each entry goes straight to the team's tracking sheet

FOR THE COMMITTEE
Signed-in VolSoc committee members also get the planning portal: the shared event plan, committee availability and the volunteer list
What you see depends on your role in the society

SIGN IN WITH UCL
Signing in uses your UCL account through Adam's Campus Toolbox, so VolSoc never sees your password
You only need to sign in to join the volunteer list or to use the committee tools; the calendar and Zero Food Waste log are open to everyone

Made by students, for Volunteering at UCL
